## Consolidation of evaluations by chatGPT

### Strengths Noted by Participants

- Instructors were patient, supportive, and helpful, especially with one-on-one assistance.
- The workshop was appreciated as a valuable learning experience.
- Many participants gained insight into working with ARC and metagenomic workflows.
- Good content and useful resources were provided.


### Main Areas for Improvement

#### Organization and Preparation
- Several participants felt the workshop was disorganized, with time lost troubleshooting scripts and installations.
- Participants suggested that scripts and slides be sent in advance to reduce confusion.
- Clearer expectations and step-by-step plans would help.

#### Pacing and Timing
- Early sessions felt slow due to setup issues, while later sessions felt rushed.
- Many participants recommended extending the workshop to 3 days instead of 2 to cover all material without hurrying.

#### Content Delivery
- Some participants felt there was too much information per session, making it hard to absorb and follow.
- Participants requested a stronger focus on concepts, tools, and clean, reproducible workflows rather than excessive detail on underlying content during technical sessions.

#### Platform Preparation
- Participants recommended that troubleshooting be completed on Mac and Windows systems ahead of time to avoid delays during the workshop.

## Actions to be taken for future workshops on shotgun metagenomics
- Share slides and scripts before the course.
- Extend the workshop duration to 3 days.
- Improve organization and flow, with clear steps.
- Allow more time for practice and script-writing guidance.
- Prepare for multi-platform compatibility in advance.
